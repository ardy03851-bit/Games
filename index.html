Mosswood

a cozy forest to wander, gather and build in

·

WASD / arrows to walk · tap the world to gather · a fixed, hand-painted view

How to play

Wander

iPad: push the thumb-stick, or tap the ground to walk there.
Desktop: WASD / arrow keys.

Gather

Tap a tree, boulder, bush or fallen log. The cube walks over if it is close enough. Trees and bushes rest for a moment after a harvest.

Build

Tap Build, pick something, then tap the ground. Green ghost = it fits. ↻ rotates, ✕ removes and refunds part of the cost.

Time

The valley drifts from noon to dusk to night — lanterns and windows light up on their own. Your progress saves in this browser.
